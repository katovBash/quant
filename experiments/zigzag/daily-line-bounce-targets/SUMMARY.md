## Гипотеза и контроль

A 1D line through two past confirmed lows or highs can identify a third-contact reversal: buy the support rejection, sell short the resistance rejection. Compare 5%/8% fixed take profit with 2x/4x the prior 14-day average true range; require the next open for fills. [Протокол](protocol.json) фиксирует правило, контроль и параметры.
